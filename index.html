Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Citizenly ("the App") is an independent educational app designed to help users study for the Canadian citizenship knowledge test. This Privacy Policy describes how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

Citizenly does not require an account and, in its current version, does not collect or transmit personal information to us. We do not request your name, email address, location, contacts, or payment information through the App.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store study progress, completed sections, practice questions, and related app data locally on your device to support its features. This information is not sent to our servers. Some progress may be stored only for the current app session. Removing the App or clearing its data may delete locally stored information.

3. Analytics, Advertising, and Third-Party Services

The current version of Citizenly does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, crash-reporting SDKs, or user-account services. We do not sell or share App user data with advertisers or data brokers.

When you download the App through Google Play, Google may process information in connection with its own services under its separate privacy policies. Such processing is outside Citizenly's direct control.

4. How We Use Information

Information stored locally is used only to provide the App's learning and practice functionality, such as displaying questions and tracking study activity.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a server-side account or personal-data database for App users. You can remove locally stored App data through your device's app settings or by uninstalling the App, subject to your device's backup and restore settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Citizenly is intended for people preparing for the Canadian citizenship knowledge test and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

7. Security

Because the current version does not transmit user study data to our servers, this information remains on the device. We recommend using your device's built-in security features. No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Citizenly's features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If future versions introduce analytics, advertising, accounts, or other data processing, this policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Contact Us

10. Independent Educational App

Citizenly is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of Canada or Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
